# v1.1 design notes (owner direction; queued behind v0.5 capital ships and the v1.0 power/shield/hull overhaul)

Source: the owner's requests, 2026-09-28. Re-ordered 2026-09-28: the combat overhaul (Power / Shield / Hull, repair kits, 6-man dungeons, fast difficulty ramp) became v1.0 (the owner asked for version 1), so this list is v1.1. It becomes the v1.1 contract and spec after v1.0 lands.

## 1. Maps: open plazas, congested routes (Arena + Warzone generators)
- **Wide-open areas:** team base plazas and central arenas.
- **Congested routes connect them:** asteroid mazes, winding corridors, and **narrows**.
  - A narrow is a gap wide enough for a normal ship but NOT for a capital hull. Normal ship diameters are about 32–44 px; capital diameters are about 40–68 px. So a narrow's width sits between a normal diameter plus margin and a 1-turret capital's diameter.
  - Cover alcoves beside the narrows let small ships snipe at capitals.
- **At least one capital-width main lane** links every base and every arena. It is longer and more exposed than the narrows, so capitals never get stranded.
- **Validation for every generated map (deterministic):**
  - every open tile is reachable by a normal hull;
  - every base, arena and objective feature is reachable by a max-size capital (scale at 5 turrets);
  - narrows really block capitals.
- **Bot navigation:** clearance grids per hull size (normal / capital), so bots route by their current radius.

## 1b. Map variants by size: Solar / Galactic / Cosmic (added 2026-09-28)
Three size classes, each available for both **PvP (Arena)** and **PvPvE (Warzone)**. The size tracks player count. Each class has its own set-piece hazards and features, built on the §1 layout rules (plazas, routes, narrows):
- **Solar** (smallest):
  - asteroid fields;
  - planets on slow orbits (solid, safe to touch);
  - one or more **suns** with a gravity field and an orbit / slingshot mechanic;
  - the **solar halo** does chip damage; flying into the sun is **instant death**.
- **Galactic** (mid):
  - several star systems;
  - **wormholes** (linked teleport pairs);
  - **nebulas** that disrupt shields;
  - **scanning arrays**: capturable or holdable structures that extend the team's scanning (radar / minimap reveal).
- **Cosmic** (largest):
  - **black holes** (gravity wells with an event horizon);
  - spiral galaxies as set dressing and terrain;
  - the biggest battlefields.
- **Constraints:**
  - Hazards are part of the deterministic sim (orbits are functions of tick, not free physics) and the client rebuilds them from the seed.
  - Bots must avoid lethal hazards.
  - Wire positions are u16 at 1/8 px, so `MAP_SIZE` < 8192. Cosmic maps may need a coarser quantization or a wider position encoding: a protocol change, so bump `PROTOCOL_VERSION`.

## 2. Capital death: shipyard, then eject or stay
- **The host:** when a capital (a host with ≥ 1 turret) is destroyed, the host pilot respawns at their base **shipyard** after a longer capital respawn timer.
- **While on that cooldown the pilot may ONLY SPECTATE.** The owner was explicit: no turreting onto other ships. The spectator view cycles through living players, starting with the capital's own crew, and shows a relaunch countdown.
- **Each turret chooses:**
  - **Eject:** respawn in their own regular ship right at the wreck (brief invulnerability, partial energy), then resume normal play.
  - **Stay with the crew:** every staying crew member cuts the capital's rebuild timer. On relaunch the crew is already docked, so the ship relaunches as a capital immediately, and the crew shares a loyalty XP bonus. Staying crew also spectate only while they wait.
- **Scope:** teams modes only, since there is no attaching in FFA. Dungeon Runner uses the party anchor as the "shipyard"; confirm the rules against lives.

## 3. Vendetta and streaks (PG: "takedown", never "kill")
- **Vendetta:** the pilot who last took you down is tagged on your screen. Paying them back gives bonus XP, a bounty bonus and a "PAYBACK!" banner.
- **Multi-takedowns** within a short window:

  | Takedowns | Banner |
  |---|---|
  | 2 | Double Down |
  | 3 | Hat Trick |
  | 4 | Quad Surge |
  | 5 | Pentastorm |
  | 6+ | Total Eclipse |

- **Streaks** without being downed:

  | Streak | Banner |
  |---|---|
  | 3 | Heating Up |
  | 5 | On Fire |
  | 7 | Supernova |
  | 10 | Unstoppable |
  | 15 | Mythic |
  | 20 | Legend of the Void |

  Each tier raises the XP boost and the bounty.
- **Streak Breaker:** a bonus for ending someone's big streak.
- **Presentation:** big neon banners, synth stingers, and an optional robotic announcer voice (browser speech synthesis; toggle in Settings, off in rooms if the host disables it).
- **Kill-feed wording:** switch to takedown wording as well, where it isn't already.

The owner approved these names ("the tags for kills and vendettas are great"), so they are final unless the owner revises them.
