== S1. Ultimate uptime (cast count per player per 10 min; charge kept on death; no gain while an ult runs where the kit says so)
==============================================================================================================
PvP model: fights ~3/min (4-10 s each), rates below are per alive-minute; 16-ship Arena DM at 16 takedowns/min total (gate >= 15).
PvE model: pve_profiles.json = recorded 0.1 s activity of each pilot in the CR swarm harness (variant C ramp, n=6 2/2/2, Veteran, 3 seeds).
policy "smart" = cast when full AND in a fight (bots/humans do not waste it in travel); "ready" = the instant it is full (upper bound).
Uptime = share of alive time the pilot has an ult active. Map-wide = 16 pilots (Arena/Warzone) or party of 6 (Dungeon).
