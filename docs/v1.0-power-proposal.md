## 1. Summary for the owner

**What changes in a fight**
- **Every ship has three meters instead of one.**
  - A **green shield circle** takes hits first. After you stop getting hit it waits a moment, then refills. It refills fastest when you stop shooting and hold still.
  - A **hull bar** under the ship is your real health. It does not come back by itself in a dungeon. Repair kits, the Artificer and the Repair Bay restore it.
  - A **gold power bar** is your reactor. Everything you do draws on it: flying, shooting, skills and recharging your shield.
- **Holding LMB + RMB + Space together overloads the ship.**
  - Everything costs 1.6× as much.
  - You fly at 75% speed and the reactor recharges 40% slower.
  - Your shield barely recharges.
  - Drain the reactor to zero and you **brown out**: about a second with no guns, no skills and no boost, your engines sputtering.
- **Heavy damage cripples the reactor.** Below 85% hull the ship loses power capacity and recharge, step by step, to −30% / −35% at critical. Repairing restores it. The sparks and sputtering are cosmetic; the power loss is real.
- **You pick a starting weapon each sortie:**
  - **Mass Driver:** a close-range shredder.
  - **Laser:** a wall-bouncing sniper beam.
  - **Phaser:** a mid-range shield stripper.
  - Hold any of them too long and they cost double, then start burning your own shield.

**The buttons (Overwatch-style)**
- **LMB** main fire. **RMB** alt fire (Rocket Salvo / Arc Lightning / Deploy Sentry).
- **E** special, on a medium cooldown:
  - Juggernaut: **Polarize Shields** (swarmers that touch you die).
  - Arcanist: **Gravity Well**.
  - Artificer: **Repair**. Tap it for a heal pulse; hold it for the **Repair / Docking Ray**, or the **Siphon Ray** on the Leech variant.
- **Q** ultimate, on a charge meter (about 2 minutes passive, about 70–80 s in real play):
  - Juggernaut: **Event Horizon**. Park, and everything is sucked into you.
  - Arcanist: **Ion Storm**.
  - Artificer: **EMP Pulse**, which also reboots allied shields.
  - Your level-3 path reshapes it.
- **Space** maneuver:
  - Hold Space with a direction to **boost**.
  - Hold Space with no direction for a **FULL STOP**.
  - Double-tap Space (or press C) for your **maneuver perk**: Ram Charge, Warp, Strafe Dodge, Overdrive or Hyperspace Jump.
- **R** drops a bomb (you always carry some), or uses a mine prize if you picked one up.

**Handling**
- The Juggernaut slides about 2.7 s if it coasts, but FULL STOP halts it in about 0.5 s.
- The Arcanist stops almost instantly, so it can stop and shoot.
- The Artificer sits in between and has the strongest reactor.
- A capital ship gets heavier with every turret it carries.

**Progress without pay-to-win**
- Perks unlock by playing matches (read from your existing match stats).
- You can also try any perk in a match through level-5 and level-10 perk cards.
- Arena gives everyone every perk by default. Perks are sidegrades, never stat boosts. Cosmetics stay cosmetic.

**XP pips**
- Every ship has gravity. Pips visibly curl and spiral in before they snap on.
- Bigger ships pull harder.
- A **Tractor Field** pickup and a rare **Vacuum** pickup boost the pull.

**Dungeon Runner**
- Parties of up to **6**.
- A **Threat** level climbs quickly with every minute on a floor and with every floor. It raises enemy count, speed, damage, fire rate, elites and trickle spawns within minutes, Geometry Wars style.
- More enemies drop repair kits, and kits dropped near you are shared with nearby allies.
- The sim checks put average bot parties of 6, bot parties of 4 and a solo Juggernaut through a 6-floor Veteran run (3/3 seeds).

**Unchanged:** the 3 classes × 3 paths plus talents, turret kits and laser resonance, the v0.5 capital ships and their skills, and cosmetic-only loot.

### 2.2 Per-tick order

Server `Sim.step`. The client predictor mirrors every step marked ✱.

**Pass 1 (movement, free ships):**
1. ✱ **Load:**
   - `L = min(3, primaryHeld + secondaryHeld + spaceSys + eStance)`.
   - `spaceSys` = 1 while boosting, or while braking at \|v\| > 5 px/s.
   - `eStance` = 1 for the Polarize sustain, the Repair Ray or the Siphon Ray.
   - Held levels come straight from the input, so the predictor gets L exactly.
   - Docked turrets count primary + secondary only.
2. ✱ **Thrust:** thrusting (\|move\| > `THRUST_DEADZONE`) pays `thrustDraw × LOAD_SURCHARGE[L] × DT`.
3. ✱ **Maneuver** = `maneuverOf(input)`.
   - **Boost** engages if not in brownout, power > one tick of its cost, and the ship is not inside a hostile Event Horizon pull (Overdrive ignores that last check).
   - Boost pays `afterburnerCostPerSec × LOAD_SURCHARGE[L] × DT`.
   - **Brake** pays `stopDraw × LOAD_SURCHARGE[L] × DT` while \|v\| > 5 px/s. It is free in brownout.
4. ✱ **Speed multiplier:** `speedMult = hostPenalty(n) × repairBoost × objSpeedMult × STRAIN_SPEED[L] × (brownout ? 0.7 : 1)`.
   - `massMult = capitalMass(n)`.
   - Then call `stepShipMovement(…, boost, speedMult, massMult, stopMode)` (§7).

**Pass 3 (reactor, weapons, skills):**

5. ✱ **Regen:** if not boosting and not EMP'd:

   ```
   power += powerRegen × reactorRechargeMult(tier) × STRAIN_REGEN[L] × turretMult × objRechargeMult × capRegenMult(n) × DT
   ```

   Then clamp to `effMaxPower = maxPower × reactorCapMult(tier) × capPowerMult(n)`.
6. **Spend.** When a skill is pressed on a tick it is debited **before** the weapon, so a held gun can never starve it (judge fix). Then:
   - weapon (§6), RMB, E, perk, R, capital skill (§5);
   - every cost × `LOAD_SURCHARGE[L]`;
   - a discrete cost is a no-op if it can't be paid in full;
   - a continuous draw takes `min(power, draw)`; if short, it cuts and trips the breaker.
   - Turret offense drawn on the host is **not** surcharged. The host pays its own L only.
7. **Shield regen** (§2.4) gets what is left.
8. ✱ **Brownout trip** (deterministic, judge fix):
   - It trips when stored power < `BROWNOUT_TRIP_FRAC` 0.02 × effMaxPower after all draws, **or** when a wanted continuous draw was refused.
   - It clears when power ≥ `BROWNOUT_RECOVER_FRAC` 0.25 × effMaxPower.
   - Emits `brownout {shipId, x, y}`.
9. Heat decay (§6.4), the ultimate meter (§4b.2), then flags.

| L (held systems) | Cost surcharge | Speed & thrust | Reactor regen (judge fix) | Shield regen |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | ×1 | ×1 | ×1 | **×1.6** |
| 1 | ×1 | ×1 | ×1 | ×1.0 |
| 2 | ×1.25 | ×0.9 | ×0.85 | ×0.5 |
| 3 (LMB + RMB + Space) | ×1.6 | ×0.75 | ×0.6 | ×0.15 |

- **Taps add no load:** E taps, Q, the perk and R. Only held systems do.
- **Low power** (0 < power < 20%): shield regen ×0.5, and the power bar pulses.
- **Brownout** (from the trip until 25%):
  - **Locked:** main fire, RMB, E, **Q**, perk, R and boost.
  - **Still works:** FULL STOP, at full strength and free.
  - Speed and thrust ×0.7.
  - Shield regen stops. Every continuous system cuts: Polarize, rays, the Laser.
  - Recovery while thrusting: Juggernaut 1.04 s, Arcanist 1.07 s, Artificer 0.63 s.

### 2.4 Shield

**Delay**
- Any damage sets `shieldReadyTick = tick + shieldDelay`.
- A hit that breaks the shield, or lands while it is at 0, uses `shieldBreakDelay`.
- **Polarize:** no delay; the shield regenerates under fire.
- **Phaser hit on a shield** (graft): +0.5 s to the target's pending delay, at most +2 s beyond its base.
- **Warp arrival:** the remaining delay is trimmed to ≤ 1.0 s (graft).

**Regen per tick**

Regen runs when `tick ≥ shieldReadyTick`, the shield is below max, and the ship is not in brownout, not EMP'd, not overheated, not boosting and not DISRUPTED.

```
rate = shieldRegen × SHIELD_LOAD[L] × fire × idle × (lowPower ? 0.5 : 1) × turretMult × capShieldRegen
fire = (tick − firedTick < 0.4 s) ? SHIELD_FIRING_MULT 0.5 : 1      // weapon or turret offense actually fired
idle = (L == 0 && !thrusting && fire == 1) ? SHIELD_IDLE_MULT 1.25 : 1
cost = gained × SHIELD_POWER_COST 0.6 (not surcharged; if short, gain = power / 0.6)
```

**Time to full after a break** (power not limiting):

| | Parked idle | L0 thrusting | L1 firing | L2 firing | L3 firing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Juggernaut | 4.9 s | 5.2 s | 9.3 s | 15.2 s | 43 s |
| Arcanist | **3.2 s** | 3.4 s | 5.6 s | 8.7 s | 24 s |
| Artificer | 3.9 s | 4.2 s | 7.3 s | 11.8 s | 33 s |

**Events and flags**
- `shieldBreak {shipId, x, y}` (presentation R4).
- `SHIPFLAG2_SHIELD_DELAY` while below max with the delay pending.
- `SHIPFLAG2_SHIELD_BROKEN` from the break until the shield regrows past 15%.
- `ShipView.shieldFrac` snaps (no lerp) across `SHIELD_BROKEN` edges (R2).

### 2.6 Damage pipeline

`damageShip(world, ship, amount, sourceShipId, cause, type: DamageType = 'energy')` stays the one entry point. Projectiles get their type from `DAMAGE_TYPE_OF_KIND`; every other caller passes one explicitly. The order:

1. Dead, or amount ≤ 0 → return. **Invulnerable** → return.
2. `'void'` (v1.1 sun or black-hole core) → integrity −1 → takedown.
3. Ram Charge: Unstoppable is immune; otherwise × `chargeDamageTaken` 0.4.
4. Entropy: ×1.3.
5. **Hardening** `h`:
   - `h` is the *strongest* of Polarize 0.5, Fortress, Anchorage, Repair Bay 0.3 and Event Horizon anchor 0.25, capped at 0.95.
   - `amount ×= 1 − h`.
   - Reflector returns 40% of the absorbed amount (reflect depth ≤ 2).
6. **Brace:** `× (1 − braceAbsorb)` with diminishing returns (§5.5).
7. **Layers:** `[sm, im] = TYPE_MULT[type]`.
   - `'emp'` and `'disrupt'` hit the shield only. `'disrupt'` also sets `shieldBlockUntil`.
   - Otherwise, if shield > 0: `sd = amount × sm`.
     - If sd ≤ shield: `shield −= sd`, and **nothing reaches the hull.** This is the readable rule: ring up = safe.
     - Else: `overflow = (sd − shield) / sm` raw, shield = 0, **break**.
   - If the shield was already 0, overflow = amount.
   - `hullDmg = overflow × im × (1 − min(armor, ARMOR_CAP) × (type == 'crush' ? 0.5 : 1))`.
   - `SHIELD_BREAK_GRACE_SEC` defaults to 0. It is a knob: if the burst gate (§13.7) measures a SHIELDS DOWN window under 0.3 s, set it to 0.3, which makes hull damage ×0.3 for that long after a break.
8. **Delay** (§2.4). `'ion'` also drains power at 0.5 × amount after hardening.
9. **Events** (presentation R5):
   - `hit {x, y, targetKind:'ship', targetId, amount, layer: 'shield' | 'hull' | 'break', dir?: u8, dtype}`, where `dir` is the angle from the victim toward the source in 256 steps and is omitted when there is no source.
   - Then `shieldBreak` if the shield broke.
   - Then `lastDamagedBy` as today.
10. `integrity < 0` → `killShip` (unchanged bookkeeping; power, shield and integrity set to 0).

## 4. Repair kits

### 4.1 Pickups ride the gem pipeline

- `Gem.kind` is one of `'xp' | 'kit' | 'bigkit' | 'bombpack' | 'minepack' | 'tractor' | 'vacuum'`.
- `dropPickup(world, x, y, kind, value?, lifeSec?)` rolls on **world.rng**, never on `lootRng`.
- **Small kit:** 15% of max hull. **Large kit:** 35% of max hull + 30% of max shield. Either restores reactor tiers at once.
- **Need-gated:** a kit is pulled (by the pip well, §4b.6) and collected only by a ship below 95% hull. A large kit also counts shield below 70%. Full ships fly through, and the kit waits for whoever needs it.
- **Shared burst** (graft): allies within 320 px of the picker (docked turrets included) get 50% of the kit's value.
- **Pity** (graft): after taking ≥ 70% of max hull in damage since your last kit, your next credited takedown drops a guaranteed small kit at the victim.
- **Lifetime:** dungeon 30 s (numsim: 20 s let 37% expire); elsewhere 15 s. Kits blink for the last 4 s.
- **Cap:** kits and prizes share `MAX_PICKUPS` 80, separate from XP's `MAX_GEMS` 600. They are **never** merged into XP; the oldest small kit is evicted first.
- **Event:** `pickup {shipId, playerId, kind, x, y}`, which drives the kit flash and chime.
- The floor swap clears pickups.

**Global rules:**

4. **Docked turret:**

     - a docked Artificer's EMP Pulse centres on its host;

### 4b.2 Ultimate charge

- **Meter:** `ship.ult` ∈ [0, 1] in the sim.
- **Wire:** `YouState.ultCharge` u8 = round(ult·254), and **255 = ready**.
- **Allies:** a team-only tail `tu` (flat `[shipId, 0..100]`) feeds the team strip, the rift roster and a ready ring on allied nameplates (G4). Opponents never receive it; bots read it only for their own team.

**Per tick, while alive and no ultimate is running:**

```
ult  += DT / ULT_PASSIVE_SEC (120)                          // passive: full in 120 s
bank  = min(ULT_BANK_CAP 0.15, bank + activity)             // activity this tick (table)
move  = min(bank, ULT_ACT_RATE 0.0085 × DT); bank −= move; ult += move     // activity feeds ≤ 0.85 %/s
ult  += instant bonuses (uncapped); ult = min(1, ult)
```

**Activity sources** (effective amounts only: damage actually applied after hardening and armor, repair actually restored; overkill and overheal are clamped):

| Source | Amount |
|---|---|

| Hot Point hold (your side scoring) | 0.005/s |
| **Never counted** | damage to deployables, friendly fire, self-damage, kits, **anything an ultimate does** |

### 4b.3 Space: boost, FULL STOP and the perk trigger

**`maneuverOf(input)` → `'boost' | 'brake' | null`** is pure and shared by the Sim and the predictor:
- **Boost:** (`mobility` or `afterburner`) held **and** \|move\| > deadzone.
  - Thrust ×1.5, cap `afterburnerSpeed`, power and shield regen halted.
  - Sets `SHIPFLAG_AFTERBURNER`, which now means "boosting" (presentation R8).
  - If the boost gate refuses, Space + direction is plain thrust, and it still counts in L.
- **Brake (FULL STOP):** `mobility` held **and** \|move\| ≤ deadzone. Shift never brakes.
  - Retro-brake (§7.1). Free and full-strength in brownout.
  - Sets `SHIPFLAG2_BRAKE` (presentation R8, the reverse flare).
  - It counts as "not thrusting" for `lan_focus`.

**Perk trigger:** `InputState.perk` is a one-tick edge. The client sets it on any of these:
- **a double-tap of Space or pad A:** a press within `PERK_DOUBLE_TAP_MS` 300 ms of a previous press that was held ≤ `PERK_TAP_MAX_MS` 200 ms, via the pure `isDoubleTap(prevDownMs, prevUpMs, nowMs)` in `input/mapping.ts`, so feathering a boost never fires a perk;
- **C** or **pad L3** (direct);
- optionally Mouse 4.

The first press of a double-tap gives just 1–3 ticks of boost or brake, so boost and brake have no input delay. A Settings toggle, "Perk on double-tap" (default on), turns the double-tap off; C and L3 always work.

### 4b.5 Unlocks and fairness

- **Starters** (everyone, guests included): the class **home perk** plus **Strafe Dodge**.
- **Persistent unlocks** (G7), derived from the existing `Profile.stats` by a pure `perkUnlocked(stats, perk, shipClass)` in `data/perks.ts`. Nothing new is stored; `PROFILE_VERSION` stays 1.
  - The other two classes' home perks: `stats.matches ≥ 5`.
  - Hyperspace Jump: `stats.byType.dungeon.matches ≥ 3`, or `stats.matches ≥ 20`.
- **In-match trials (Perk cards):**
  - At levels **5 and 10** (`PERK_CARD_LEVELS`), one of the three cards is a Perk card for a perk you haven't equipped, drawn by `world.rng` among the rest.
  - Taking it equips that perk at once, with a 3 s lockout, and adds it to `perkChoices` for this match.
  - When every perk is already available (perkRule `'all'`), that card becomes a general card.
- **Room rule** `RoomSettings.perkRule: 'unlocked' | 'all' | 'signature'` (G6):
  - Defaults: `'all'` in Arena and Arena house rooms, which makes pure PvP identical for every account; `'unlocked'` in Warzone and Dungeon.
  - `'signature'` (home perk only) is the competitive option.
- **Online guests:** starters plus in-match cards (`'all'` rooms give everything). **Offline:** the device profile.
- **Sidegrades only.** No perk or unlock raises damage, hull, shield, power or charge rate. Cosmetic loot never grants perks; the `engine` cosmetic may restyle boost, brake and perk trails only.
- **No persistent XP-pull perk:** pull is a progression advantage.
- **Balance gate:** `objectiveParity` with random bot perks. No perk may shift a class's Deathmatch takedown share by more than ±10%.

### 4b.6 XP pip gravity and pull powerups (G1)

Shared pure math in `sim/gravity.ts` (also used by wells, Event Horizon, blackholes and v1.1 black holes). Stepped in `pve/gems.ts`.

```

R   = WELL_BASE × √(r0/18) × (1 + 0.12·n) × (1 + 0.20·magnetLv) × pullMult      // WELL_BASE 240 PvE / 190 Arena; R ≤ 900
C   = shipRadius + CAPTURE_PAD 60 + 4·magnetLv       (capture zone: today's snap homing, want = max(520, shipSpeed + 350), k = min(1, 9·dt))
col = shipRadius + 10                                (collected)
A   = 1900 × √capitalMass(n) × (1 + 0.25·magnetLv) × pullMult
in the well (C < d ≤ R), in the owner's frame:  u = (d − C)/(R − C);  a = A·(1 − u) + 320 toward the ship
   swirl: tangential 1.1·a·max(0, 1 − tOwned/1.1 s), sign = side of current relative motion (pip-id parity if at rest)
   damping: only the tangential relative velocity, at (2 + 4·tOwned)/s;  relative speed ≤ 700 px/s
outside every well: today's friction (0.08^dt), then the pip sleeps
```

## 5. Classes

`hpScale(e) = e.maxHp / (ENEMY_DEFS[e.kind].hp × (e.elite ? ELITE_HP_MULT : 1))`: the tier × party × Threat × difficulty × instability multiplier the enemy spawned with. **Every PvE number from a skill, an ultimate or an item is × hpScale.** Which column applies (vs enemies or vs ships) depends on the **target**, never on the mode.

### 6.3 Laser reflection

- **Trace:** the pure `traceBeam(world, ship, out)` in `sim/targeting.ts`.
  - It starts at the nose along `ship.angle`.
  - `raycastWallN` (the DDA extended to return the hit point and face normal) finds each wall. Shield Walls are tested with the blocker sweep. `firstHitOnSegment` finds bodies.
- **Bounces:** 2 base, +1 per Multi-fire level, +1 with Lance; hard max 4.
  - Each reflection off a tile or a **Reinforced** Shield Wall keeps 75% of the remaining range, with damage ×0.8.
  - A normal Shield Wall **absorbs** the beam, taking its damage. This closes the v0.5 beams-through-walls gap.
- **Self-hit rule:** a beam never hits its owner, the owner's host, or any turret on the owner or its host, even after a bounce and even with friendly fire on.
- **Length:** total path length, capped at `MAX_VIEW_HALF_EXTENT` 1500 with every bonus.
- **Wire:** a beam that bounced sends its exact vertices in the JSON tail `bm` (flat `[shipId, n, x1, y1, …]`, interest-filtered). Clients never re-trace remote beams.
- **Event Horizon** does not bend beams.

## 7. Inertia per class

### 7.1 Movement model

`sim/movement.ts` is pure and shared by the server and the predictor:

```
stepShipMovement(body, input, stats, map, dt, boost, speedMult, massMult = 1, stop: 'none' | 'brake' | 'anchor' = 'none')
thrusting:  v += dir·thrust·speedMult·(boost ? 1.5 : 1)/m·dt
            grip: the off-thrust component w = v − max(v·dir, 0)·dir bleeds |w| ← max(0, |w| − (drag·grip·|w| + brake·grip)/m·dt)
            HARD CLAMP to (boost ? afterburnerSpeed : maxSpeed)·speedMult   (judge fix; also fixes today's afterburner creep)
coasting:   |v| ← max(0, |v|·(1 − drag/m·dt) − brake/m·dt)
'brake':    |v| ← max(0, |v|·(1 − drag/m·dt) − (brake + stopDecel)/m·dt)        (FULL STOP; full strength in brownout)
'anchor':   move ignored; v ×= (1 − 10·dt)                                       (Event Horizon, Jump spool)
overspeed from impulses only: bleeds at OVERSPEED_IMPULSE_DECEL 2500 px/s²;  walls: restitution 0.5
m = massMult = capitalMass(turretCount) on both hosts (derived; never scaled twice)
```

- `MOVE_DRAG` is retired. `stopDistance(v, drag, brake)` is exported for bots; pass `brake + stopDecel` for FULL STOP.
- **Knockback impulses** are × `knockTaken / m`. **Ability pulls** are ÷ m (§0.19).

### 7.2 Stops

Tick-accurate, to below 1 px/s (`tables.mjs` §A):

| Class | Cruise | Coast from cruise | **FULL STOP** | Counter-thrust | FULL STOP vs coast | From boost speed: coast → STOP | 0 → 90% cruise | Cruise → boost cap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Juggernaut | 430 | 2.67 s / 435 px | **0.53 s / 105 px** | 0.67 s / 138 px | ×5.0 faster | 2.75 s / 476 px → 0.60 s / 139 px | 0.63 s | 0.23 s |
| Arcanist | 500 | 0.23 s / 46 px | **0.12 s / 20 px** | 0.15 s / 31 px | ×2.0 | 0.28 s / 75 px → 0.15 s / 39 px | 0.42 s | 0.17 s |
| Artificer | 470 | 0.88 s / 166 px | **0.28 s / 58 px** | 0.40 s / 86 px | ×3.1 | 0.95 s / 203 px → 0.33 s / 85 px | 0.48 s | 0.18 s |

**Capitals** (host penalty plus mass; coast → FULL STOP from the reduced cruise):

| | n = 1 | n = 3 | n = 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dreadnought | 3.22 s / 493 px → **0.63 s / 116 px** | 4.12 s / 550 px → 0.77 s / 121 px | **4.72 s / 535 px** → 0.83 s / 109 px |

- **FULL STOP beats counter-thrust on every hull, n = 0..5** (RF2 gate).
- **Stop-and-pop:** an Arcanist's cruise → stop → shot → cruise cycle takes about 1.0 s; a Juggernaut's about 1.6 s.
- **Shoves:** a 500 px/s shove slides a Juggernaut about 200 px while coasting and about 34 px while braking.

### 7.3 Prediction

- New `ShipStats` fields (`drag`, `brake`, `grip`, `knockTaken`, `stopDecel`, `stopDraw`) reach the client through `you.stats` (`STAT_KEY_TABLE`).
- `maneuverOf`, L and the brake are pure functions of the replayed input. `massMult` comes from `turretCount`.
- **The predictor's power model:**
  - regen × `reactorRechargeMult(hullDamageTier(you.hull/you.hullMax))` × `STRAIN_REGEN[L]`;
  - thrust, boost and brake draws, and per-shot primary costs;
  - brownout hysteresis;
  - re-synced from `you.power` / `you.brownout` every snapshot.
- **Predicted:** perks that move the ship (Ram, Warp, Dodge, Overdrive, the Jump spool and arrival) and the Event Horizon anchor (own `ULT` / `ANCHORED` flags plus the local press; the Blink `localUse` precedent).
- **Not predicted** (as today): knockback, pulls, EMP.

## 10. Bots

- **Three meters:** `efOf` becomes `shieldFrac` / `hullFrac` / `powerFrac`.
- **Fight on the shield, retreat on the hull:**
  - `retreatFrac` (hull): Juggernaut 0.30, Arcanist 0.45, Artificer 0.40. `recoverFrac` 0.75.
  - With shield at 0 and hull below 60%: kite at max weapon range.
- **DamageMeter** is rebuilt from the previous tick's `hit` / `shieldBreak` events aimed at the bot (graft). That is deterministic and removes the latent `mobilityCost` vs `capCost` bug.
- **Loadout:** §6.6.

**Range and movement**
- **Engage bands:**

  | Weapon | Band |
  |---|---|
  | Mass Driver | 120–260 px |
  | Phaser | 350–600 px |
  | Laser | 700 px to (range − 80) px |

- Aim and lead use `gunRange` / `gunSpeed`. The Laser is handled as hitscan.
- **Targets:** Phaser bots prefer high-shield targets; Mass Driver bots prefer SHIELD_BROKEN ones.
- **Inertia-aware steering:** `arrive()` holds Space with no direction (FULL STOP) once `dist ≤ stopDistance(v, drag, brake + stopDecel) / m + 20 px`. Whiskers look ahead by the stop distance. Juggernaut and capital bots brake when a whisker finds a wall inside their stop distance above 250 px/s.
- **Stop-and-pop:** Laser bots brake when the target is in band with line of sight, fire while stopped, and move again if a hostile comes within 250 px.

**Power and heat discipline**
- Never hold 3 systems, except to finish a target below 25% hull.
- Hold RMB only if power > 50% or heat < 40.
- Release Space once \|v\| < 20 px/s.
- Heat feather: 95 / 70 (Phaser: vent aware).
- **Boost:** only at power > 45%, and only when fleeing below the retreat line, chasing a target below 30% out of range, carrying an objective, or when the goal is > 900 px away. Cut at 25% power.

## 14. Risks and open questions

### 14.1 Risks

1. **Contract churn stacked on the v0.5 code.** This covers:
   - the `energy` → `power` rename (about 449 hits in 55 files);
   - three new input bits, with Space and E becoming levels;
   - the capital cooldown moving from `mobilityReadyTick` / `SPACE_CD_TICKS` to `utilityReadyTick` / `utilCdTicks`;
   - flags2 as u16 and a 40 B ship record.

   All of it must land in the one PROTOCOL 6 bump, or codec, bots and prediction break twice. M1 carries the whole contract.
2. **Ultimate noise vs "game-changing moments."** About 12 ultimates a minute in a 16-ship Arena. Knobs: `ULT_PASSIVE_SEC`, `ULT_ACT_RATE`, and the ult economy gate.
3. **The PvE ramp was validated without ultimates.** RF9's instant ring kills plus about one ultimate per pilot per minute may trivialise floors 1–3, or the added Threat may overshoot on Nightmare. The RF14 gates, `RIFT_ULT_ALLOWANCE`, the Event Horizon crush fallback and the solo ×0.8 knob are the controls. Solo Arcanist and Artificer are the weakest profiles.
4. **Prediction at the brownout edge.** Brownout and boost-gate transitions between 20 Hz snapshots, plus longer unpredicted-impulse corrections on low-drag hulls and capitals, can rubber-band. Watch `SNAP_DIST` in playtests.
5. **Input and teaching.**
   - The gamepad remap (alt fire to LT, attach to X, ultimate on Y) breaks v0.5 muscle memory.
   - A double-tap can misfire on a pad. The 200 ms tap rule, the Settings toggle and C / L3 mitigate it.
   - Players must learn load, heat, the E tap/hold grammar and the Q lock reasons.
   - The phone HUD needs its 6-tile re-check.
6. **Scope and performance.**
   - 6 bots with 350 enemies, Ion Storm arcs, Event Horizon pulls and pip gravity must fit the 4 ms tick.
   - Nine ultimate modifiers, five perks, bot ultimate heuristics and wards make a large AI and FX surface.
   - Cut order if late: wards → surge pulses → mines → Hyperspace Jump → the Arena prize field.

### 14.2 Questions for the owner

1. **Bomb run.** Bombs are standard issue on R, and the Juggernaut's ultimate is Event Horizon. Do you want a *Carpet Bomb* ultimate as the Barrage path's instead? The cost is the "one ultimate per class" simplicity.
2. **Architect.** The Architect trades Deploy Sentry for a 2-charge Shield Wall on RMB, so Summoner and Medic Artificers have no wall. OK?
3. **Capital skill on E.** Per your rule the capital skill replaces E. A hosting Dreadnought loses Polarize, a Spire loses Gravity Well, and a Foundry keeps Repair (tap = Repair Bay, hold = Ray). Confirm?
4. **Docked ultimates.** May docked turret pilots fire their ultimate as specced (Ion Storm from the dome, EMP centred on the host, Event Horizon undocks first), or should Q be disabled while docked?
5. **Ultimate pace.** An average pilot gets one about every 75–80 s in Arena, and 66–82 s in dungeons (120 s passive). Faster (for example 100 s passive, about 65 s in play) or slower?
### 14.3 Owner decisions (2026-09-28)

1. **Bomb run:** keep **one ultimate per class**. Every Juggernaut's Q is Event Horizon, and the Barrage path only reshapes it. Bombs stay standard issue on R. There is no Carpet Bomb ultimate.
2. **Architect:** no objection raised. The spec's 2-charge Shield Wall on RMB stands.
3. **Capital skill on E:** the owner's rule stands. While hosting, the capital skill replaces E (a Dreadnought loses Polarize, a Spire loses Gravity Well, a Foundry keeps Repair as tap = Repair Bay / hold = Ray).
4. **Docked ultimates:** **allowed, as specced.** Ion Storm fires from the dome, EMP centres on the host, and Event Horizon undocks the pilot first.
