# v1.0 design notes: Power / Shield / Hull overhaul (owner direction, 2026-09-28)

The owner asked for this release to be **version 1.0.0**. It is a MAJOR bump: the combat model and the wire protocol change incompatibly.

Queued behind v0.5 (hardpoints and capital ships). The detailed spec is being designed (three competing designs, numeric sims, a judge and a synthesis) and will be saved as `docs/v1.0-power-proposal.md`.

Owner requirements:
1. **Dungeon Runner:** max party of **6** (was 4), with a difficulty that ramps up **quickly**, Geometry Wars style.
2. **Combat must feel much more dangerous.** Replace "energy = health = ammo" with three meters:
   - **Shield:** after it drops you can take a few direct hits, and it powers back up after a short delay. It recharges faster when you aren't firing.
   - **Hull (integrity):** takes the hits once the shield is down.
   - **Power:** moving, firing and recharging the shield all draw on it.
3. **Repair kits** drop as things die, and more often than now.
4. **Juggernaut:** much more armor, fine to sit in a fight, and a **Polarize Shields** ability that damages anything that runs into it.
5. **Engineer:** repairs itself and others quickly with a **docking / repair ray** that draws power. A variant has a **Siphon** ability.
6. **Power draw as risk/reward:** LMB, RMB and Space all pull more power while in use. Holding all three together taxes the ship's systems, so it moves slower and its shield recharges slower.
7. **Weapons have range, and you pick a starting weapon** (added 2026-09-28):
   - **Mass Driver:** physical damage, falls off with distance (more damage the closer you are), high rate of fire. Sustained fire overheats it: it draws more power, then starts taking from the shield.
   - **Laser:** sniper range, sustained beam, **reflects off walls**. Held too long it starts drawing from the shield.
   - **Phaser:** mid-range, **strips shields drastically**, medium hull damage.
   - The choice is made per sortie (class select / hangar), independent of the class. It replaces or augments the base auto-weapon; the design decides how it interacts with class skills and turret kits.
8. **Inertia gameplay** (added 2026-09-28): ships keep sliding until they slow. How long depends on the class:
   - **Juggernaut** (large, tanky): slides longest and takes longest to slow.
   - **Arcanist** (small, fast): stops almost instantly, enabling stop-and-pop sniping.
   - **Artificer** (engineer): in between, with a higher engine / power recharge rate.
   - Capital ships (hosting turrets) should carry even more momentum.
9. **New armaments** (added 2026-09-28), SubSpace-style prizes/items on an item key:
   - **Bombs:** area damage.
   - **EMP Pulse:** knocks out shields and power.
   - **Ion Storm:** a crackling electric area field.
   - **Singularity:** the ship becomes an event horizon. Everything nearby is pulled toward it and crushed while it holds position.
   - The design decides how items are acquired (drops / caches / loadout) and how they are balanced in PvP vs PvE.
10. **Ship readouts** (added 2026-09-28), shown on every ship in the world, not only the HUD:
    - **Shield:** a **green circle around the ship** showing one overall shield level. No fore/aft/side facings.
    - **Hull:** a **health bar underneath the ship** for hull integrity.
    - **Power:** shown in some other easy-to-read way, so the player can infer power at a glance. The ship's systems are power, weapons, special, shields and thrusters.
    - **Power routing is AUTOMATIC for every system.** The owner, 2026-09-28: "keep it simple and frantic". One reactor; whatever you use draws from it. There is no manual pips or power-routing mechanic.
11. **Adaptive music** (added 2026-09-28):
    - Sparse, almost minimalistic, until you start leveling up and racking up takedowns.
    - Adjusts with the number of things on screen.
    - Peaks as full-on techno synthwave chiptune with a frantic but heroic beat.
    - When your hull gets low it turns dramatic, with an **alarm sound built into the music**.
12. **Damage feedback** (added 2026-09-28):
    - **Screen:** at low hull the screen starts to turn red, with **cracks along the periphery**.
    - **Ships:** show **5 levels of physical damage** as they lose hull: sparks and debris flying off, thrusters shorting out, guns shorting out and throwing sparks.
    - **Heavy damage costs real power** (owner, 2026-09-28): "the more damage, the more of a hit the power takes and the less faster it recharges."
      - The damage tier (hull below 85 / 70 / 50 / 30 / 15% = tiers 1–5, one shared function) lowers the ship's max power and its power recharge.
      - Repairs restore both.
      - The power bar shows the lost capacity.
      - The sparks, debris and sputtering thrusters and guns are **cosmetic only**.
    - The detailed design is in `docs/v1.0-presentation-proposal.md`.
13. **Ability layout, Overwatch-style** (owner, 2026-09-28):

    | Input | Role |
    |---|---|
    | LMB | **main fire** (your chosen starting weapon) |
    | RMB | **alt fire** (class alt fire; fast) |
    | E | **special** (medium cooldown) |
    | Q | **ultimate** (long cooldown; recommended: an Overwatch-style charge meter fed by damage, healing and takedowns) |
    | Space | **maneuver:** hold with a direction = max thrust (replaces the Shift afterburner); no direction = full stop; plus an equipped maneuver perk (warp, jump, dash, ...) |

    - **Maneuver perks** unlock in play and in persistent character data, as sidegrades (options, not raw power), so PvP stays fair.
    - **Ultimates:** the new armaments (Event Horizon, Ion Storm, EMP Pulse, Bomb run) become ultimates where they fit. Leftovers become pickups on R.
    - **Capital ships:** the capital skill replaces E while hosting.
14. **XP pip gravity** (owner, 2026-09-28): every ship has a gravity well that pulls XP pips in. Pips visibly curve and spiral before they snap in. Today it is a hard 120 px snap.
    - **Modifiers:** the Magnet card (reworked) plus pull **powerups** such as a timed Tractor Field and a rare Vacuum that pulls every pip on screen.
15. **Keep:** everything that already exists (classes, paths, turret kits, resonance, capital ships, cosmetic-only loot, PG wording).

The v1.1 queue (narrow-route maps, capital shipyard respawn with spectate-only waiting, crew eject/stay, vendetta and streaks) is in `docs/v1.1-notes.md`.
